Safety Message

Pedestrian Safety

Maximize safety on the road by following these Transport Canada guidelines:

  • Stay vigilant — be alert as both a driver and a pedestrian at all times
  • Avoid distractions — keep your full focus on the road while driving
  • Low-visibility conditions — stay extra alert at night and in hard-to-see weather
  • Obey traffic signs and signals — always yield to pedestrians in crosswalks

TSPA Fuel Mileage Settlement Refresher

  • Fuel mileage settlement fluctuates with the price of diesel fuel and is indexed on a weekly basis.
  • The settlement is based on static fuel tables included in the TSPA, specifying the rate paid out based on the current cost of fuel.
  • These tables account for higher or lower settlement rates as the cost of fuel increases or decreases.
